## OPTIONS/HOLDS

You can quickly send quotes and place holds on boats.

**Important:**
- **Limit holds (options) to 1-2 boats per location per client** to avoid imposed rental restrictions
- Options default to **3-day duty-free holds**
- Contact brokercare@dreamyachtcharter.com to request a limited extension

## BOOKINGS

Email **brokercare@dreamyachtcharter.com** to convert a quote or option into a booking.

This will alert after-sales to send your 'Welcome' email, which includes:
- Requirements to sail at that destination
- Payment information
- Booking confirmation & contract
- Base guide
- All other necessary documents

## PAYMENTS

### Wire/ACH Instructions
Navigare and Dream Yacht Wire/ACH instructions are at the bottom of every contract and confirmation.

### Discounts
- **Automatic:** Promotions/special offers and early booking discounts are automatically applied by the reservation system
- **Manual:** Some discounts can be applied manually; available discounts are listed in the 'Conditions & Extras' in the agent portal under 'Useful Information' per destination

### Commission Structure
- **Net / Gross:** Clients pay you 'total client price' (gross) on contract, you pay us 'agency price' (net) on confirmation, keeping the difference for commission

### Payment Schedule
- **30% deposit** due immediately after a booking is confirmed
- **Final payment** due 60 days ahead (per contract)

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Checks
- Debit
- Wire/ACH
- Credit card (5% commission reduction applies)
