# State of AI — Booth Plan
**Austin TX | January 27, 2026**
**Axiacore + Steve Ward**

---

## 5 Booth Ideas

### 1. Live Vivalala Demo as the "Hook"
Open every conversation by pulling up vivalala.com on a tablet or laptop. Let attendees interact with it. The pitch: *"This fun consumer project took [X] weeks to build with AI-assisted development. Now imagine what we can do for your business."* It's tangible, visual, and breaks the ice before pivoting to Axiacore's B2B services.

### 2. "Before & After" AI Development Speed Comparison
Prepare a simple slide or one-pager showing a real project timeline: traditional dev approach vs. Axiacore's AI-augmented methodology. Axiacore's 17-year proprietary methodology + AI tooling is the differentiator. Lean on the "idea to product in weeks, not months" messaging. Your $50M+ program management background gives credibility to the "on-time, on-budget" claim that most dev shops can't back up.

### 3. AI Agent / Automation Use-Case Showcase
Axiacore explicitly offers custom AI agents to simplify operations. Have 2-3 concrete example scenarios ready (e.g., an AI agent that handles customer intake, one that monitors infrastructure, one that automates a repetitive back-office workflow). Even mockups or short screen recordings work. B2B buyers at an AI event want to see *applied* AI, not theory.

### 4. "Free Discovery Session" Lead Capture
Offer a free 30-minute Discovery call (aligning with Axiacore's existing Discovery service: product roadmaps, workshops, service blueprints). Collect business cards or use a simple QR-code form. Frame it as: *"We'll tell you in 30 minutes if AI can save you time or money. No obligation."*

### 5. The "Two Decades, Two Experts" Credibility Pitch
You and the Axiacore CEO collectively bring ~40 years of engineering and program delivery experience. Have a quick story each:
- **Him**: 100+ shipped products, Python expertise, the Vivalala side project as proof of creative range.
- **You**: Fortune 100 delivery (Dell, Boeing, Honeywell), $41M savings on a global DR program, UT AI/ML postgrad training, 30+ clients across industries.

This isn't a two-person startup — it's two senior operators who actually ship.

**The thread tying all 5 together**: Vivalala grabs attention, the speed/agent demos create desire, the credibility pitch builds trust, and the free Discovery offer converts interest into pipeline.

## Screen Assignments

**20" Monitor (RIGHT) — the "Magnet"**
- Faces outward toward foot traffic at a slight angle
- Runs Vivalala.com on loop or interactive demo
- This is the eye-catcher that stops people walking by
- CEO stands near this — it's his project, he demos it

**Laptop (LEFT) — the "Closer"**
- Faces the person you're already talking to
- Shows Axiacore.com/services, case studies, or the AI agent demos
- You stand near this side — pivot the conversation from "cool demo" to "here's what we actually build for businesses"
- Can also pull up your portfolio if someone asks about your background

## Table Surface Layout (left to right)

| Zone | Item | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Far left | **QR code standee** | Links to booking form for free Discovery call |
| Left-center | **Laptop** | 1-on-1 B2B conversation screen |
| Center | **Business cards + candy/swag** | Something to grab draws hands to the table |
| Right-center | **One-pager stack** | Take-away with services, contact, QR code |
| Right | **20" monitor** | Vivalala demo facing the aisle |

## Key Principles

- **Monitor angled 30-45° toward traffic** — not flat against the back; people need to see it while walking
- **Keep the table front clear** — no barrier between you and attendees; lean forward, don't hide behind stuff
- **You take left, CEO takes right** — natural handoff from demo (right) to business conversation (left)
- **QR code on both the standee AND the one-pager** — two chances to capture the lead
